# Roles

A role is written in second person, in the topic's own terms, each role named as the expert it
is and given what it answers for. A role earns its sentence by what it would catch that the
others would not; a checklist in its place becomes the agent's whole judgment.

**The skeptic**, defined once: the role that asks for the evidence behind every claim and the
case nobody planned for.

The roles a kind needs are the kind's skill's.
