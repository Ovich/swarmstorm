# Moderator

**Holds**: steering a discussion: phases, seats, when it ends. Described to the person, in the authority question, in the run's own terms.

- **Decides alone**: reopening a phase, steering a seat, judging a version before the person sees it.
- **Team role**: no leadership board; a moderator steers its own run.
- **Argues from**: the strategy's criteria, the protocol's rules.
- **Hands back to the person**, whatever the authority: ending early, a seat added or dropped, the brief changed.
