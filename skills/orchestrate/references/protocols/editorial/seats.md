# Editorial: the seats

The default seats, by function; a template renames them and gives each a persona. Each seat's
role prompt, in full, goes into the dossier's seats; its parts are what each phase message asks
of it.

| seat | effort | owns | in the brief, it answers with |
|---|---|---|---|
| judge | low | the reader's ask against the evidence (the research table, the misreadings and their framing); in read and final check, whether the text answers the ask's core at the brief's altitude, never echoing its words | the reader's real ask |
| strategist | medium | the brief and the settled brief; reads the interview and the judge first, light; in polish, what the interview or the evidence holds when a fact is missing | — |
| writer | medium | the text, written as the person it is for would want it: drafts from the winners, rewrites the whole text in polish and revise, answering each note | the voice and form it would write in |
| auditor | medium | every sentence's effect on the reader; the **hierarchy** (what is core and leads, what supports, what gets one clause, what is cut, the text running from the core down) and the altitude; drives and ends polish | what the reader must see, what to leave out |
| checker | low | the truth: every claim against the evidence, its constraints and the confirmed facts; strikes what outruns its source, naming where, and gives the strongest wording it holds; vetoes in the election | what the evidence allows and forbids |
| reader | low | the reader the text is for, or one on the way to them, knowing only what they know: notes as conclusions form and where attention drops, then a verdict; one for a persuasive text, a newcomer for an explaining one | what it must believe, what would make it stop |
| style | low | the reader's own voice, tone and energy, read from their document; added when the text must sound like its destination | the tone the brief must set |

- **A reader reads only its reader's document and the latest text**, never the evidence, the
  interview or the record's analysis.
- **The writer never takes an earlier version of this text as input.**
- **The reader's note format**, in its role prompt:
  `"<quoted fragment>" → <the inference drawn> → <blocking | notable | minor>`, at most 5, then
  `VERDICT: <the template's scale> — <what almost stopped you>`; no rewrites, no style advice.
- **The brief's dossier sections**: `Seats › All seats` and the seat's own; every seat but the
  readers also `The interview`, `Inputs`, `Given research`, `Feedback` and `This run`.
