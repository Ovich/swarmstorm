# Idea-to-outcome: the documents

All three go to aiview (the `aiview` skill), in the run's group, kind `report`.

**The record**, one per run: the goal and the yardstick; the baseline table; then one section per
idea: its note, each round's judged tables, the result (before → after), the person's verdict and
decisions; any protocol change the person made, dated; at the close, one table of every idea and
its state, what remains before a release, and the lessons worth keeping.

**The design note**, posted by d1 and copied into the record:

- *For the agents*: what changes for the orchestrator and the members, in their words.
- *Problem*: the evidence, with numbers and sources.
- *Decision*: the design, a diagram when it is quicker than prose.
- *Rejected*: one line each, why.
- *Acceptance criteria*: each measurable, its owner (a verifier or the tester), its metric.
- *Tasks*: id, owner, files, done-when, boundary, and the merge order.
- *Risks*, *Out of scope*, and the decisions left to the person with a recommendation each.

**The review page**, one per idea, read in a minute: what changed, for whom · why (the evidence) ·
verified live, as a short table · the numbers, including the ones that did not improve · what
stays open · the decision, and the person's other choices, each with a recommendation.
