# Brainstorm: the documents

**The record** is the `brainstorm` skill's board, one per run, its sections as that skill
orders them: the strategy under its human part, the questions as its decisions rows, the
options and their pre-mortems written in as each phase closes, the debate log last.

**A dossier** only when the person will run the topic again: then the strategy, the given
research and the person's feedback move out of the board into
`YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-dossier.reference.md` (`## The strategy`, `## Given research`,
`## Feedback`), and each new run's board names it. A single run keeps them on its board.
