# The reflect phase

**A building block any run with a cycle can add, of any kind (build-verify passes, brainstorm or
debate rounds, editorial revisions), when the loop circles instead of converging**: the work
paused, every voice asked once what is going on, the answers synthesised, the person deciding how
to go on. A loop whose every pass meets the same kind of fault again is fixed by its cause, not by
one more pass; the members holding the work see that cause, each a different part of it.

## When

- **The person asks** ("let's reflect"): a charter amendment (`references/charter.md`), run before
  the next piece of work.
- **You judge it**: from the third pass of one kind on (a phase's kind is the word its name opens with; the
  `phase` verb's answer reminds you every third pass), ask
  yourself *closer or circling?*, from a table across the passes:

  | pass | what stood against the goal | same kind as an earlier pass |
  |---|---|---|

  Signs of circling, not rules: the same kind of fault or objection in two passes or more in a row;
  a fix that passed its own check and failed where the work is used; one part reopened pass after
  pass; decisions changing under the members. Seen, **propose a reflect to the person in one line, with the
  table**, and open it on a yes; with a `pace` that gives you the run, open it and say so.

## The phase

1. **Pause**: every member keeps its work in progress where it is (a builder commits it on its
   branch and merges nothing); the phase text names the paused tasks.
2. **One post per voice**, every member, you and the person's session when it takes part,
   `--about D-reflect`, in five parts:
   - **closer or circling**, with evidence: finding ids, counts per pass;
   - **why**: the cause, from the voice's own part of the work, with where it is (a path, a post);
   - **structure**: what to change in how the work is built or framed;
   - **flow**: what to change in what its user does or sees, or in how the run itself proceeds;
   - **stop**: what to stop doing.
3. **Synthesis**: you write it once every voice has posted, in an aiview report (kind `report`): the
   verdict, the cause the voices share, the top changes ranked with their cost, what to stop. Its
   link on the board.
4. **Architecture round** (optional, `reflect-arch`): when the synthesis proposes a structural
   change, post the proposal; each member answers against its own part, `holds` / `breaks` /
   `missing` / `change`, each with where. Rank the objections into the synthesis. A proposal
   checked only by its author misses what each member's part knows.
5. **The person decides**, in one decisions post: resume the loop, amended; or an architecture phase
   (`references/architecture-phase.md`) then the work; or the close. Until then no pass opens.
   **The next check is set where the faults lived**: what passed alone and failed where it is used
   is re-checked as it is used.

**A member with nothing to add posts so**, in one line, and `done reflect`.

## Phase message

| phase | text |
|---|---|
| reflect | `reflect: the loop is paused (<paused tasks>), commit your work, merge nothing. Each of you posts once, --about D-reflect: closer or circling (with evidence), why (with where), structure, flow, stop. Then done reflect.` |
| reflect-arch | `reflect-arch: the proposal is <post or doc>. Each of you answers against your own part: holds / breaks / missing / change, each with where, --about D-reflect-arch. Then done reflect-arch.` |
