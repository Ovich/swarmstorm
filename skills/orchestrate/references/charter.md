# The charter

**The charter is the approved proposal** (`SKILL.md`, *Propose the run*), adjusted where the person
adjusted it; a field the kind's skill asks in its own message (a plan's phases, seats and verify)
is asked there. Write the answers into the kind's document, in its charter
section, as `field: value` lines, and into its one line, where the kind keeps one. **Only the
person amends the charter**: a field you think should change is a pause, with your reason, never
a decision of yours. An amendment mid-work overwrites its field in both places, the decision's id
beside it (`pace: run through, the person merges (D41)`), and reaches the next brief only.

**Every kind's charter holds**, in this order:

- `protocol:` the base it is built on, and its changes, each with its reason;
- `run:` the run table, as approved;
- `loop:` when the protocol has a cycle: `until <goal> · cap <n>`, `<n>`, or `unbounded`;
- `design:` `none` (the default), `d1` (a designer seat) or `orchestrator`: a design phase opens the
  run, as `references/design-phase.md` says, when the person asks for it; with it, `design scope:`;
- `effort:` each seat's model and effort, a column of the run table: **Opus for every seat**;
  **`medium` for a seat that thinks** (designs, judges or argues: a designer, a verifier, a
  reviewer, a brainswarm seat), **`low` for a seat that carries work out** (a builder, a tester);
  the person changes any row, or all. Passed at every dispatch, as *Dispatch* says; never left to
  a subagent definition's own level unasked;
- `authority:` what the person delegates, per role, asked as `references/authority.md` says.

Then the protocol's fields, then the kind's: their order and recommendations are their files'.
