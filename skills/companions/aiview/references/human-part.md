# The human part

The head of a document a person must grasp in minutes, someone who was not there included.
It says nothing the sections below do not back. In this order:

1. **Brief**: what is being thought through and why, and the constraints; enough for a reader
   who was not there.
2. **The idea**, in prose. Every sentence ends on its traces: the ids it rests on, `(D3, Q7)`,
   a diagram's caption, or a recipe in *What ran*. **A sentence with no trace is a finding.**
   Traces are ids in text, not links.
3. **Diagrams illustrating the idea**: as many as the idea needs, each with its caption line
   and its traces, drawn through the `write-diagrams` skill.
4. **For you**: what waits on the person, in plain words: the open and deferred rows, and every
   ruling to confirm or overturn, each in one line with both sides and its id.

**The document's writer refreshes it at each settled decision**; it is never left behind the
rows.
